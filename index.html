Three navigation approaches

All three methods are evaluated on the same episode, each shown through three simulation views and a live trajectory plot.

01

Nominal-Flow

The baseline flow-based navigation method, providing a reference for comparing the guided approaches on the same episode.

02

Q-Guided-Flow

An RL-critic-guided flow navigation method that uses a learned critic to guide the generated actions.

03 Proposed methodology

Cost-Flow

Our approach combines generative flow matching, predictive robot dynamics, and mathematical cost objectives. In this demonstrated episode, Cost-Flow recovers where the other two methods fail.

How Cost-Flow works

The method connects generated robot actions with predicted future motion and a mathematical cost function.

01 · Generative

Flow matching

Generates a candidate trajectory of 32 future robot actions.

02 · Predictive

Robot dynamics model

Uses the current robot state and generated action trajectory to predict future states.

03 · Mathematical

Cost objectives

Evaluates the predicted future trajectory using a mathematical cost function.
